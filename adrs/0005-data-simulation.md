# ADR 0005: MQTT simulation with explicit provenance\n\n## Status\nAccepted.\n\n## Decision\nFive deterministic-configurable simulated stations publish measurement/status topics. Payloads include message_id, timezone timestamp and `source=simulator`. Consumer validates, deduplicates and persists; status/freshness are backend-derived.\n\n## Consequences\nDemo is reproducible and transparent; data cannot be called official/live certified. Invalid/stale samples remain diagnostic only.\n\n## Verification\nNormal, spike, duplicate, malformed, silence and reconnect scenarios.
