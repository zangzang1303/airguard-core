# ADR 0003: Rule-owned alerts and mandatory HITL\n\n## Status\nAccepted; exact threshold/cooldown pending Mentor confirmation.\n\n## Decision\nBackend Rule Engine evaluates only valid, fresh data. Alerts are deduplicated by station/rule and record rule version. Agent may create an evidence-based `pending` proposal but cannot approve/reject. Only manager role may transition pending to approved/rejected; dispatch occurs only after server-side approval. Every transition is audited.\n\n## Consequences\nSafer demo and explainable decision trail; more API/state tests. Stale/offline/invalid data must block proposals and alerts.\n\n## Verification\nBoundary, duplicate, stale, reject, concurrent-review and dispatch-failure tests.
